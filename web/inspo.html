About Quartr

IR material – the primary input in qualitative company research – had never been structured or made queryable at scale. Analysts accessed it through scattered IR pages, clunky terminals, and manual effort. The information existed. The infrastructure to work with it didn't.

Today Quartr leads globally in coverage of live public company events across 65+ markets – delivering structured, real-time IR data through two offerings: Quartr Pro, a research platform for investment professionals, and Quartr API, the data infrastructure for builders.

More than 800 of the world's most demanding financial institutions and technology companies rely on Quartr daily, from hedge funds and asset managers to the largest AI companies in the world.
